Frequently Asked Questions

Everythhing you need to know about GamePlanner+.

Is GamePlanner+ free to use?

Yes! GamePlanner+ is completely free to use. You can organize your game collections, track your progress and discover new games without paying anything.

Of course! You can create custom collections, add your favorite games and organize them however you like. You can also edit or delete your collections at any time.

You can log your gaming sessions, record how many hours you've played and add notes about your progress. This helps you keep track of your gaming journey.
